CORTEX — Certificate of Experience on Semiconductor Training Readiness Through Experiential Learning — connects coursework, laboratory training, and an industry capstone at Colorado School of Mines. The project brings together Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, and Physics.

## Three areas of work

1. **Certificate development.** Define the pathway with educational and industry input, using relevant existing courses.
2. **Capstone supervision.** Work with industry partners to provide semiconductor-related project experience.
3. **Tools integration.** Adapt SMILE training tools to equipment in the Mines cleanroom and integrate them into laboratory instruction.

## Year 1 plan

These are planned activities from the project summary, rather than a report of completed milestones.

### Through September 2026

Define the certificate scope with educational and industry experts. Prepare a public program page and adapt SMILE detection models using images and videos of Mines cleanroom equipment.

### October – December 2026

Integrate and test interactive SMILE tools in the Mines Nanofabrication Facility. Identify industry capstone partners.

### January – March 2027

Deliver the first offering of the revised microelectronics processing laboratory course and supervise capstone projects.

### April – June 2027

Collect student feedback, evaluate outcomes and partnerships, and prepare course materials for sharing through NNME’s openly licensed digital repository.

## Planned outcomes

The Year 1 project summary sets targets of **15 learners**, **10 credentials awarded**, **one modernized course**, and **six graduates entering semiconductor careers**. These figures are program goals, not measured results.

## Sharing materials

The project plans to share curriculum and training modules with consortium partners through NNME. A repository link will be added when the materials are available. In the meantime, the [SMILE browser tools]({{ '/smile/' | relative_url }}) are linked on this site.
