From coursework to practice

The program brings together Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, and Physics. Students apply what they learn in the lab and through work with a semiconductor industry partner.

01

Engineering & computing

Build a foundation through existing courses in semiconductor devices, digital design, materials, physics, and computing.

02

Hands-on training

Learn microelectronics processing with laboratory work and SMILE interactive videos, virtual tours, and augmented reality tools.

03

Industry experience

Complete a qualifying capstone project with a semiconductor partner, or discuss an approved alternative with the program team.

SMILE — Semiconductor Manufacturing via Innovative Learning Experience — supports cleanroom training in a browser, in virtual reality, and on a HoloLens 2 headset. Start with an interactive video or explore the 360° cleanroom tour.

A Mines effort within NNME Southwest

The certificate is supported by the National Network for Microelectronics Education (NNME) Southwest Node. The project develops the certificate, supports industry capstone work, and integrates SMILE tools into microelectronics laboratory instruction.
