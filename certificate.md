CORTEX combines existing courses with laboratory training and semiconductor industry experience. It is offered by Computer Science in collaboration with Electrical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering, and Physics. There is no added tuition for the certificate.

## Who can apply

Mines juniors and seniors enrolled in **EDNS 491 and EDNS 492 in Fall 2026 or Spring 2027** are eligible to apply. Contact the program team to confirm whether your project, courses, or an alternative experience qualifies.

<a class="button" href="https://tinyurl.com/SemiconductorCert">Certificate application form</a>

## Requirements: 12 credits

### Industry experience · 6 credits

Complete **EDNS 491 and EDNS 492** with a semiconductor project or role.

An alternative pathway may use a 3-credit MME, PHYS, or CBE capstone with a semiconductor industry partner, or an industry internship, together with an approved elective. Discuss alternatives with the program team before planning your coursework.

### Hands-on component · 3 credits

Choose one of the following:

- **PHGN 435** — Microelectronics Processing Laboratory
- **EENG 532**
- **EENG 484** — Advanced Digital Design

### Engineering & computing elective · 3 credits

Choose an approved course from:

- **Electrical Engineering:** EENG 421, EENG 423
- **Computer Science:** CSCI 410, CSCI 564, CSCI 582
- **Physics:** PHGN 317, PHGN 441, PHGN 550
- **Metallurgical & Materials Engineering:** MTGN 315

Other courses may qualify with approval.

<div class="note">Confirm your course selection and capstone eligibility with the program team. Check course availability and approval before enrollment.</div>
